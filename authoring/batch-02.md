# Authoring batch 02 — Wave 3 — planets & major asteroids

Marks: ✓ lands · ~ flat · ✗ wrong/preachy. React, don't analyse.

## body-sun · body-intro
KERNEL: The Sun is the centre of conscious identity and life-purpose — the “I” that chooses. Its sign shows how you become yourself; its house shows where.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The Sun is the centre of conscious identity — the "I" that chooses, and the life-purpose that keeps recruiting the rest of the psyche. Its sign shows how becoming yourself is done; its house shows where. | B:
MUNDANE: The Sun marks what you organise your life around: vitality, visible identity, the roles where you must be someone rather than do something. | B:
ENERGY: The Sun is the system’s power source — the current every other circuit ultimately draws on. Its condition sets the whole chart’s baseline amplitude. | B:
MINIMAL: Core identity and purpose. Everything else orbits this. | B:
MARKERS: identity, purpose | ASK: Where in your life do you feel most unmistakably yourself?

## body-moon · body-intro
KERNEL: The Moon is instinct, need and the felt past — how you self-soothe and what makes you feel safe. Fastest-moving, it colours daily mood.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The Moon is the feeling nature and the inner child: what safety felt like first, and what the psyche still reaches for under stress. It runs on memory, not argument. | B:
MUNDANE: The Moon is daily emotional weather — moods, appetites, home habits, who and what feels comforting. It shows what you need, as distinct from what you want. | B:
ENERGY: The Moon is the fastest-moving current: intake, tide, replenishment. Its sign shows how the system refuels; its aspects show what interferes with refuelling. | B:
MINIMAL: Needs, moods, comfort. Non-negotiable, pre-rational. | B:
MARKERS: need, instinct | ASK: What do you reach for when you want to feel safe?

## body-mercury · body-intro
KERNEL: Mercury is how you think, learn and put things into words — perception, speech, and the traffic of the near mind.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Mercury is the messenger function — how experience gets named, connected and told. It carries the psyche’s translations between inner and outer. | B:
MUNDANE: Mercury covers speech, writing, learning, errands, siblings, and the everyday exchange of information. | B:
ENERGY: Mercury is the signal layer: bandwidth, routing, noise. Aspects to it change what gets through and how distorted. | B:
MINIMAL: Thinking and communicating. The interface. | B:
MARKERS: mind, speech | ASK: Do you think by talking it out, or by going quiet?

## body-venus · body-intro
KERNEL: Venus is what you value and how you attract — taste, affection, and the terms on which you relate and enjoy.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Venus is the valuing function: what the psyche finds beautiful, worthy and worth drawing close. In relationship it shows how affection is offered and recognised. | B:
MUNDANE: Venus covers love, taste, pleasure, money-as-enjoyment, and the manners of relating — how you attract and what you appreciate. | B:
ENERGY: Venus is the attracting current — it pulls rather than pushes. Its condition sets how easily the system draws in what it values. | B:
MINIMAL: Attraction, values, affection. What you say yes to. | B:
MARKERS: value, attraction | ASK: What do you find beautiful that others around you don’t?

## body-mars · body-intro
KERNEL: Mars is desire in action — how you assert, pursue and fight; the body’s will and its temper.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Mars is the will in action: desire that moves, anger that defends, the courage to separate and to want. Undeveloped, it either erupts or goes limp; developed, it is clean assertion. | B:
MUNDANE: Mars covers drive, competition, conflict style, physical energy and how you go after what you want. | B:
ENERGY: Mars is the discharge current — bursts, thrust, ignition. Its aspects show whether output fires cleanly or backfires. | B:
MINIMAL: Drive and aggression. How you push. | B:
MARKERS: desire, assertion | ASK: How do you behave when you finally lose patience?

## body-jupiter · body-intro
KERNEL: Jupiter is where you expand, trust and seek meaning — faith, opportunity, and the appetite for more.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Jupiter is the principle of meaning and growth — the inner teacher that says life is larger than this. It shows where faith comes naturally and where excess does too. | B:
MUNDANE: Jupiter covers opportunity, luck, travel, education, publishing and everything that expands your world — including waistlines and budgets. | B:
ENERGY: Jupiter is the amplifier: whatever current it touches, it turns up. Generosity and overreach are the same dial. | B:
MINIMAL: Growth, optimism, excess. Where more happens. | B:
MARKERS: expansion, meaning | ASK: Where does life tend to open up for you without much forcing?

## body-saturn · body-intro
KERNEL: Saturn is limit, structure and time — where you meet difficulty, take responsibility, and slowly earn mastery.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Saturn is the reality principle and the inner elder: limit, consequence, earned authority. What it touches matures late and lasts. Its lessons repeat until taken personally. | B:
MUNDANE: Saturn covers duty, structure, career ceilings and floors, delays that turn out to be curricula, and the rewards of sticking at things. | B:
ENERGY: Saturn is impedance and containment — it narrows current to deepen it. Pressure here is load-bearing strength being built. | B:
MINIMAL: Limits, discipline, time. Where the work is. | B:
MARKERS: limit, mastery | ASK: What have you got good at only by doing it badly for years?

## body-uranus · body-intro
KERNEL: Uranus is the disrupting principle — sudden insight, independence, and the break from what has settled.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Uranus is the awakener: the part of the psyche that must be free and true even at the cost of belonging. It breaks what has become automatic. | B:
MUNDANE: Uranus covers sudden change, technology, rebellion, and the places life refuses to stay settled. | B:
ENERGY: Uranus is high-frequency interruption — surges, static, lightning. It resets circuits that have rusted closed. | B:
MINIMAL: Disruption and freedom. Where you won’t comply. | B:
MARKERS: disruption, independence | ASK: Where do you refuse to be predictable?

## body-neptune · body-intro
KERNEL: Neptune dissolves boundaries — imagination, longing and compassion, and the fog of what cannot be measured.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Neptune dissolves the ego’s boundaries: imagination, longing for the infinite, compassion — and equally glamour, fog and self-deception. It shows where you idealise. | B:
MUNDANE: Neptune covers dreams, art, spirituality, escapism, and areas of life that stay chronically hard to pin down. | B:
ENERGY: Neptune is diffusion: current spreading into mist. What it touches gains atmosphere and loses edges. | B:
MINIMAL: Imagination and fog. Where edges blur. | B:
MARKERS: dissolution, longing | ASK: Where do you lose your edges — for better or worse?

## body-pluto · body-intro
KERNEL: Pluto is depth and transformation — power, compulsion, loss, and what is remade by being destroyed.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Pluto is the depths: buried material, compulsion, power and the psyche’s capacity to die into a truer form. What it touches cannot stay superficial. | B:
MUNDANE: Pluto covers crises that transform, power struggles, shared resources, and the areas of life that go through death-and-rebirth cycles. | B:
ENERGY: Pluto is the deep reservoir current — slow, immense pressure that periodically forces total renewal of the channel. | B:
MINIMAL: Depth, power, transformation. All or nothing. | B:
MARKERS: depth, transformation | ASK: What in you has been rebuilt by something you didn’t choose?

## body-chiron · body-intro
KERNEL: Chiron is the wound that instructs — where injury and healing meet, and where you help others through what you cannot fully cure in yourself.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Chiron is the wounded healer: an early injury that never fully closes, which becomes — precisely through conscious tending — the place you can guide others. Wound, coping, gift: that arc. | B:
MUNDANE: Chiron shows a sore spot that keeps resurfacing as real situations, and the mentoring or healing role that eventually grows there. | B:
ENERGY: Chiron is a lesion in the field that the system keeps routing around — each conscious pass lays down stronger tissue than unbroken wire would have. | B:
MINIMAL: The recurring wound and the skill it teaches. | B:
MARKERS: wound, healing | ASK: What do you help others with that still aches in you?

## body-vesta · body-intro
KERNEL: Vesta is focused devotion — the flame kept for a chosen work, and what you hold sacred and undivided.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Vesta is the hearth-keeper: devotion, focus, the sacred flame tended in privacy. It shows where wholeness comes from dedication rather than relationship. | B:
MUNDANE: Vesta covers focused work, service, and the practices kept up faithfully — the desk, the studio, the altar. | B:
ENERGY: Vesta is the pilot light: a small, constant, protected flame the rest of the system relies on. | B:
MINIMAL: Devotion and focus. The tended flame. | B:
MARKERS: devotion, focus | ASK: What would you protect time for, whatever else was asked of you?

## body-pallas · body-intro
KERNEL: Pallas is applied intelligence — pattern-recognition, strategy and craft brought to bear on a problem.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Pallas Athene is pattern-wisdom: strategy born whole from the head, creative intelligence that sees the design in the chaos — often carrying a theme of talent in a father’s world. | B:
MUNDANE: Pallas covers strategy, problem-solving, crafts and politics — where you win by seeing the pattern first. | B:
ENERGY: Pallas is the pattern-recognition circuit: it converts noise into design. Aspects show what feeds or jams it. | B:
MINIMAL: Strategy and pattern-seeing. | B:
MARKERS: strategy, pattern | ASK: What kind of problem do you see the shape of before others do?
