# Authoring batch 00 — Wave 0 — sign axes & inflections (the frame)

Marks: ✓ lands · ~ flat · ✗ wrong/preachy. React, don't analyse.

## axis-aries-libra · sign-axis
KERNEL: Aries and Libra are one axis: the self that acts and the other who answers. Aries asserts a life by starting things; Libra makes life liveable by weighing and relating. Without each other, will has no counterpart and harmony has nobody home.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The first axis of the zodiac sets the ego's oldest question: how to be someone without being alone. Aries carries the raw 'I am'; Libra carries the discovered 'you exist' — and whichever pole is lived, the other tends to arrive from outside, worn by partners and opponents until it is recognised as one's own. | B:
MUNDANE: In daily life this axis runs between initiative and negotiation: starting things, versus keeping the peace among the people they affect. Strong Aries placements push ahead and apologise later; strong Libra placements consult first and can wait too long — most careers and marriages need a working exchange rate between the two. | B:
ENERGY: A fast, sparking current meets a poising one: thrust and counterweight on the same wire. When the pair works, action stays graceful and diplomacy keeps its nerve; when it splits, the system alternates between collision and stalemate. | B:
MINIMAL: Self versus other, the first axis. Aries starts; Libra weighs. Overdone: combat, or appeasement. | B:
MARKERS: direct, relational | ASK: Under pressure, do you act first or consult first — and what has that cost?

## axis-taurus-scorpio · sign-axis
KERNEL: Taurus and Scorpio are one axis about substance: what is mine and can be kept, against what is shared, merged and inevitably changed. Taurus holds and stabilises; Scorpio penetrates and transforms. Security without depth goes inert; intensity without ground consumes itself.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: This is the axis of possession and surrender: the ego's need to have and hold, set against the psyche's need to merge and be remade. What Taurus builds, Scorpio composts — and the personality matures by discovering that letting go is not the loss of substance but its transformation. | B:
MUNDANE: Money, bodies and belongings live here: earning and keeping on one side; inheritance, debt, sex and shared resources on the other. Comfortable routines meet the crises that redistribute everything — the practical art is owning things without being owned by them. | B:
ENERGY: A slow, accumulating charge meets a concentrated, discharging one — the battery and the lightning. Held together they give staying power that can also regenerate; split apart, one pole hoards while the other burns the stores down. | B:
MINIMAL: Keeping versus transforming. Taurus holds; Scorpio burns through. Both are about what matters. | B:
MARKERS: steady, intense | ASK: What are you keeping safe that might be asking to change?

## axis-gemini-sagittarius · sign-axis
KERNEL: Gemini and Sagittarius are one axis of mind: the near view and the far view. Gemini gathers particulars — names, facts, conversations; Sagittarius wants the pattern that makes them mean something. Facts without meaning scatter; meaning without facts inflates.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The near mind and the far mind are one function split in two: curiosity that collects, and conviction that connects. Each pole shadows the other — the collector secretly longs for a creed, and the believer quietly fears the detail that doesn't fit. | B:
MUNDANE: Errands, messages and local knowledge sit at one end; travel, study, publishing and belief at the other. The axis shows in how someone learns and teaches — quick versatile exchange, or the long argument toward a worldview — and good teachers work both ends of it. | B:
ENERGY: A darting, branching current meets a long-arcing one: signal traffic against trajectory. Together they make thought that travels, detail feeding direction; apart, the system chatters without going anywhere, or launches without checking the map. | B:
MINIMAL: Near mind, far mind. Gemini collects the facts; Sagittarius wants their meaning. Each corrects the other. | B:
MARKERS: curious, far-ranging | ASK: Which do you trust first — the telling detail, or the big picture?

## axis-cancer-capricorn · sign-axis
KERNEL: Cancer and Capricorn are one axis between private roots and public role: where you come from and are held, against what you build and answer for. Cancer protects the inner, remembered world; Capricorn structures the outer, accountable one. The parental inheritance rides on both ends.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: This is the parental axis: the remembered mother-world of belonging and the father-world of demand, however the actual parents distributed the parts. The adult task is to stop commuting between them — to build a life in which being held and being responsible are no longer opposites. | B:
MUNDANE: Home and career sit at either end, and time given to one is felt at the other — the oldest work-life ledger there is. Strength at the Cancer end makes places that hold people; strength at the Capricorn end builds things that outlast them; either way, the family past tends to set the terms of ambition. | B:
ENERGY: A tidal, containing current meets a slow crystallising one: the spring and the masonry. In cooperation, feeling supplies the reason for structure and structure gives feeling somewhere safe to live; in conflict, the home drains the career or the career hollows the home. | B:
MINIMAL: Roots versus role: the parental axis. Cancer holds; Capricorn builds. Each funds the other. | B:
MARKERS: protective, ambitious | ASK: When work and home pull at once, which wins by default — and who decided that?

## axis-leo-aquarius · sign-axis
KERNEL: Leo and Aquarius are one axis between the individual and the collective: the heart that must shine as itself, and the mind that belongs to something larger than any self. Leo personalises; Aquarius generalises. Warmth without perspective turns theatrical; perspective without warmth turns cold.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The king and the citizen share one throne: the need to be singular, and the need to be one among equals. Whichever pole the ego claims, the other returns as critique — the performer haunted by the group's judgement, the reformer secretly hungry to be special. | B:
MUNDANE: Creative work, romance and children sit at one end; friends, causes and networks at the other. Leo-weighted people lead from the front and take things personally; Aquarius-weighted people organise, theorise and keep a cooler distance — a team needs both its star and its system. | B:
ENERGY: A radiant central current meets a distributing one — the sun and the grid. Aligned, personal fire lights common purposes; opposed, charisma competes with the collective, or the collective flattens the spark. | B:
MINIMAL: The heart versus the group. Leo shines as itself; Aquarius joins the pattern. Both are loyal, differently. | B:
MARKERS: expressive, independent | ASK: Where do you most want recognition — as unrepeatable, or as truly one of us?

## axis-virgo-pisces · sign-axis
KERNEL: Virgo and Pisces are one axis between order and ocean: the discriminating craft that makes daily life work, and the dissolving compassion that makes it bearable. Virgo separates and refines; Pisces merges and forgives. This is the axis of service and sacrifice.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Discrimination and dissolution are one movement of the soul: sorting the world into what serves, then surrendering the sorter. Virgo's precision guards against Pisces' undertow, and Pisces' mercy redeems Virgo's verdicts — competence and grace quarrel here until they marry. | B:
MUNDANE: Routines, health and useful work sit at one end; retreat, imagination and the unbudgeted hours at the other. Virgo-strong lives run on lists and improvements, Pisces-strong ones on tides and exceptions — the workable version keeps a clean desk with the door left open. | B:
ENERGY: A fine filtering current meets a boundless one: the sieve and the sea. In rhythm, inspiration arrives and gets built into usable form; out of rhythm, the system tightens into worry or leaks into fog. | B:
MINIMAL: Order versus ocean. Virgo refines; Pisces dissolves. Service is the shared verb. | B:
MARKERS: precise, porous | ASK: What do you tidy when you're anxious — and what do you let blur?

## sign-aries · sign-inflection
KERNEL: Aries is the igniting pole of the Aries–Libra axis: direct, pioneering, quick to start and quick to burn clean. What it lacks by design — weighing, pairing, the other's view — lives across the wheel in Libra.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Aries carries the archetype of the beginner: identity proven by daring, anger near the surface and quickly spent. Its shadow is the unconsidered other — Libra — who returns as the partner demanding consultation just when momentum feels sweetest. | B:
MUNDANE: First to volunteer, first to act, best in the opening move and bored by maintenance. Life tends to hire a Libra — the partner or colleague whose role is to say 'wait, let's ask them first'. | B:
ENERGY: Cardinal fire: an ignition current — sharp rise, short arc. It moves first and thinks in motion; balance is not native here but borrowed from the opposite pole, and the borrowing steadies the burn. | B:
MINIMAL: Direct, pioneering, fast to ignite. Libra across the axis supplies the weighing. | B:
MARKERS: direct, pioneering | ASK: What happens to your fire when someone says 'let's think about it'?

## sign-taurus · sign-inflection
KERNEL: Taurus is the holding pole of the Taurus–Scorpio axis: steady, embodied, loyal to the tangible and the proven. Its complement is Scorpio — the depth and intensity it prefers not to disturb.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Taurus incarnates the conserving instinct: peace through possession, calm through the body, change admitted only on its own schedule. The refused pole is Scorpio's depth — and it returns as the crisis or the passion that finally moves what patience had settled. | B:
MUNDANE: Reliable with money, food, objects and time; slow to commit and slower to quit. The weak spot is inertia dressed as contentment — upheavals tend to arrive from outside because they were never scheduled from within. | B:
ENERGY: Fixed earth: a dense, slow-charging current with enormous holding capacity. It resists surges — which keeps life stable, and makes real transformation feel like threat before it feels like renewal. | B:
MINIMAL: Steady, embodied, keeps what it has. Scorpio across the axis brings the depths. | B:
MARKERS: steady, embodied | ASK: What would have to happen before you willingly let something go?

## sign-gemini · sign-inflection
KERNEL: Gemini is the gathering pole of the Gemini–Sagittarius axis: curious, versatile, alive in words and quick connections. Its complement is Sagittarius — the single meaning it keeps deferring.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Gemini lives the archetype of the messenger: everything is interesting, nothing is final, and the self stays light by staying plural. The deferred pole is Sagittarian conviction — committing to one meaning — and it returns as restlessness whenever cleverness starts tasting thin. | B:
MUNDANE: Good at questions, introductions, errands and first drafts; allergic to the long haul of one big answer. Careers thrive on variety, and the corrective usually arrives as a nagging sense that all these threads ought to add up to something. | B:
ENERGY: Mutable air: a branching signal current — many channels, low load on each. It refreshes fast and scatters fast; direction has to be chosen from the far pole, because it will not emerge from more input. | B:
MINIMAL: Curious, versatile, spoken aloud. Sagittarius opposite holds the question of meaning. | B:
MARKERS: curious, versatile | ASK: Which of your many threads would hurt most to drop?

## sign-cancer · sign-inflection
KERNEL: Cancer is the rooting pole of the Cancer–Capricorn axis: protective, tidal, oriented to home, memory and belonging. Its complement is Capricorn — the public structure it shelters from.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Cancer carries the mother-world: feeling as first knowledge, the past as living tissue, care as identity. Its unlived pole is Capricorn's impersonal demand — and it surfaces as the discovery that protecting someone is not the same as preparing them. | B:
MUNDANE: Makes a home wherever it lands — of houses, teams, kitchens; remembers birthdays and slights with equal fidelity. When hurt it withdraws rather than negotiates, which others read as moodiness rather than the self-protection it is. | B:
ENERGY: Cardinal water: a tidal current, advancing and withdrawing on feeling's schedule. Containment is its gift; the Capricorn pole adds the walls that keep their shape when the tide is out. | B:
MINIMAL: Protective, tidal, rooted in the remembered. Capricorn opposite builds the outer wall. | B:
MARKERS: protective, tidal | ASK: Who is allowed inside the shell — and what did it take them?

## sign-leo · sign-inflection
KERNEL: Leo is the radiant pole of the Leo–Aquarius axis: expressive, proud, ruled by the heart's need to be seen truly. Its complement is Aquarius — the cool wide view that relativises any king.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Leo lives the archetype of the sovereign child: to exist is to shine, and love must be personal or it does not count. The refused pole is Aquarian detachment — being one among many — which returns as the audience's independence: they applaud, then go home to their own lives. | B:
MUNDANE: Generous, warm, at its best given a stage and a cause it can champion in person. Criticism lands as dethronement; the corrective is the committee, the peer group — anywhere applause is replaced by parity. | B:
ENERGY: Fixed fire: a steady radiant core, warmth broadcast from a centre that must not be doubted. It powers rooms and projects alike; the Aquarian pole routes that heat outward into the shared grid before it curdles into display. | B:
MINIMAL: Expressive, proud, heart-led. Aquarius opposite asks it to be one of many. | B:
MARKERS: expressive, proud | ASK: What changes in you when nobody in the room is watching?

## sign-virgo · sign-inflection
KERNEL: Virgo is the refining pole of the Virgo–Pisces axis: precise, useful, devoted to making things work and then work better. Its complement is Pisces — the unsorted whole its standards keep at bay.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Virgo serves the archetype of the craftsman-priest: purity through work, worth through usefulness, anxiety managed by improvement. The banished pole is Piscean surrender — letting things be imperfect and whole — which returns as exhaustion before a task that was never finishable. | B:
MUNDANE: The one who reads the instructions, fixes the process and notices the error nobody else saw; also prone to doing the whole team's worrying. Relief tends to arrive from the far pole — enforced rest, a mess that turned out fine, help accepted late. | B:
ENERGY: Mutable earth: a fine discriminating current that sorts as it flows. Precision is its power and its drain; the Pisces pole restores by un-sorting — sleep, water, music, mercy. | B:
MINIMAL: Precise, useful, self-critical. Pisces opposite forgives the unfinished. | B:
MARKERS: precise, useful | ASK: What are you still improving that might already be good enough?

## sign-libra · sign-inflection
KERNEL: Libra is the relating pole of the Aries–Libra axis: balancing, fairness-seeking, incomplete without a counterpart to weigh against. Its complement is Aries — the blunt self-assertion it polishes away.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Libra carries the archetype of the diplomat: reality is a relationship, and truth must be beautiful as well as right. Its disowned pole is Aries' plain want — and it returns as the strange fury that follows years of being reasonable. | B:
MUNDANE: Excellent in negotiation, design and any room that needs cooling; slow with menus, job offers and ultimatums. Others may quietly be recruited to do the deciding — the corrective is naming a preference first, unpolished. | B:
ENERGY: Cardinal air: a poising current, always adjusting toward equilibrium between live charges. Graceful under load, it stalls when both pans are full; Aries across the axis supplies the tie-breaking spark. | B:
MINIMAL: Balancing, relational, fairness first. Aries opposite owns the blunt want. | B:
MARKERS: relational, balancing | ASK: What do you actually want, before anyone else has voted?

## sign-scorpio · sign-inflection
KERNEL: Scorpio is the transforming pole of the Taurus–Scorpio axis: intense, guarded, committed to what is real beneath the surface. Its complement is Taurus — the calm possession it cannot quite trust.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Scorpio lives the archetype of the initiate: nothing casual, nothing forgotten, love and power studied in the dark. Its refused pole is Taurean simplicity — resting in what one has — which returns as the suspicion that peace must be hiding something. | B:
MUNDANE: Loyal to a few, formidable in crisis, suited to research, therapy, emergencies and audits. Ordinary sunlit sharing is the harder discipline — not every dinner needs an undertow. | B:
ENERGY: Fixed water: a compressed current under high pressure, discharging in depth rather than breadth. It regenerates through crisis; the Taurus pole teaches slow charging — stability as a form of power rather than its absence. | B:
MINIMAL: Intense, guarded, all-or-nothing. Taurus opposite rests in the tangible. | B:
MARKERS: intense, guarded | ASK: What might it feel like to enjoy something without testing it?

## sign-sagittarius · sign-inflection
KERNEL: Sagittarius is the far-seeking pole of the Gemini–Sagittarius axis: wide-ranging, blunt, hungry for meaning and the journey toward it. Its complement is Gemini — the near detail that checks the grand arc.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Sagittarius carries the archetype of the pilgrim: life must mean something, and the meaning is always over the next hill. Its neglected pole is Gemini's patient particulars — and they return as the awkward facts the great theory forgot to include. | B:
MUNDANE: Teaches, travels, promotes and bets big; promises tend to arrive before the diary has been consulted. The corrective is the detail pass — dates, names, small print — done before the leap rather than after it. | B:
ENERGY: Mutable fire: a long-arc current, launched at the horizon and impatient of drag. Momentum is its medicine and its blind spot; the Gemini pole feeds in course corrections mid-flight. | B:
MINIMAL: Far-ranging, blunt, meaning-hungry. Gemini opposite keeps the facts in view. | B:
MARKERS: far-ranging, candid | ASK: Which inconvenient detail is your current big picture leaving out?

## sign-capricorn · sign-inflection
KERNEL: Capricorn is the building pole of the Cancer–Capricorn axis: ambitious, enduring, ordered by responsibility and the long climb. Its complement is Cancer — the softness it postpones.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Capricorn lives the archetype of the elder: worth is earned, feeling is scheduled, and the summit is expected to justify the frost. Its exiled pole is Cancerian need — being held rather than useful — which returns at the top of the mountain, asking who all this was for. | B:
MUNDANE: Delivers, endures, runs things; holidays and retirement are its known hazards. The corrective is unproductive belonging — time with people who neither report to it nor grade it. | B:
ENERGY: Cardinal earth: a crystallising current, pressure applied over years until structure forms. It bears loads others refuse; the Cancer pole keeps the stone warm, routing feeling in before the structure sets empty. | B:
MINIMAL: Ambitious, enduring, duty-bound. Cancer opposite holds the reason why. | B:
MARKERS: ambitious, enduring | ASK: If nothing needed managing tonight, what would you do with the evening?

## sign-aquarius · sign-inflection
KERNEL: Aquarius is the collective pole of the Leo–Aquarius axis: independent, systemic, loyal to principles and the wide pattern. Its complement is Leo — the personal heart it keeps at arm's length.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Aquarius carries the archetype of the future's citizen: seeing the pattern, standing a little outside the very groups it serves. Its disowned pole is Leo's personal fire — wanting to matter as oneself — which returns as the outsider's quiet ache to be, just once, the exception. | B:
MUNDANE: Strong with systems, causes, networks and reform; vaguer with birthdays, bedsides and 'how are you, really'. The corrective is showing up in person — one heart at a time rather than a mailing list. | B:
ENERGY: Fixed air: a distributed current, stable across the whole grid, cool at any single node. It connects without merging; the Leo pole reminds the network that every node is somebody's sun. | B:
MINIMAL: Independent, systemic, future-facing. Leo opposite insists the personal counts. | B:
MARKERS: independent, systemic | ASK: Which one person gets the warmth you usually give the cause?

## sign-pisces · sign-inflection
KERNEL: Pisces is the dissolving pole of the Virgo–Pisces axis: porous, compassionate, at home in what cannot be measured. Its complement is Virgo — the edge and order it keeps forgetting.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Pisces lives the archetype of the mystic: boundaries are provisional, suffering is shared tissue, and the self is happiest slightly dissolved. Its missing pole is Virgo's discrimination — this not that, mine not yours — which returns as the hard lesson that mercy without limits drowns the merciful. | B:
MUNDANE: Kind to strangers, permeable to moods, gifted in art and care and anywhere the rulebook fails; paperwork, deadlines and saying no all need scaffolding. One working routine kept sacred is the raft this ocean asks for. | B:
ENERGY: Mutable water: an oceanic current — no fixed banks, everything eventually connected. It restores others by absorption and pays for it in blur; the Virgo pole filters, giving form as a kindness to the formless. | B:
MINIMAL: Porous, compassionate, edges optional. Virgo opposite lends the working routine. | B:
MARKERS: porous, compassionate | ASK: Where does your compassion need a boundary to survive?
