# Authoring batch 01 — Wave 1 — house axes & inflections (the frame, houses)

Marks: ✓ lands · ~ flat · ✗ wrong/preachy. React, don't analyse.

## axis-h1-h7 · house-axis
KERNEL: The 1st and 7th are the angular axis of self and other — the horizon line itself. The 1st house (below it, personal) is the face I lead with; the 7th (above it, relational) is the partner, opponent, mirror. Each completes the other: the 7th shows what the 1st left out, and the 1st is what I bring to every 'you'. Angular — first impressions and first relationships land hard and early.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Ascendant and descendant are the first projection: what the 1st house cannot own turns up wearing another face in the 7th. Maturity is meeting it there and slowly taking it back. | B:
MUNDANE: Your manner, body and approach sit at one pole; marriage, close partners and declared opponents at the other. People are drawn or repelled by the first, and build a shared life through the second. | B:
ENERGY: A current strung across the horizon, immediate and high-load: what rises here is seen at once, and the bonds it forms carry the charge of first contact. | B:
MINIMAL: Self and other, across the horizon. The 1st leads; the 7th mirrors. Angular — lands early. | B:
MARKERS: self, other, angular | ASK: Which of your own qualities keep arriving in the people you partner with — or clash with?

## axis-h2-h8 · house-axis
KERNEL: The 2nd and 8th are the succedent axis of substance — what steadies and what transforms. The 2nd house (personal) is what is mine: money, body, the felt sense of my own worth; the 8th (transpersonal) is what is shared, owed, inherited and finally changed. Each completes the other: the 8th tests what the 2nd was clinging to, and the 2nd gives the 8th a self solid enough to merge without vanishing. Succedent — it holds, banks, and slowly reckons.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Self-worth built alone in the 2nd meets, in the 8th, everything that cannot be held alone — intimacy, debt, death, the other's power. The work is to own a value that survives being merged. | B:
MUNDANE: Earning, keeping and valuing sit at one pole; joint finances, inheritance, sex and crisis at the other. Comfort meets the forces that redistribute it. | B:
ENERGY: A banked, accumulating current that periodically discharges: resource stored at the 2nd, transformed under pressure at the 8th. | B:
MINIMAL: Mine and ours, kept and transformed. Succedent — it holds, then reckons. | B:
MARKERS: worth, shared, succedent | ASK: What do you treat as securely yours that intimacy or crisis has asked you to share or let go?

## axis-h3-h9 · house-axis
KERNEL: The 3rd and 9th are the cadent axis of mind — the near view and the far. The 3rd house (personal) is the immediate: siblings, streets, errands, the facts to hand; the 9th (transpersonal) is the distant: travel, belief, the meaning that ties facts together. Each completes the other: the 3rd keeps the 9th's convictions honest, and the 9th gives the 3rd's chatter somewhere to go. Cadent — it gathers, connects, and passes knowledge on.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The curious local mind and the questing far mind are one faculty divided: information in search of a worldview, conviction needing the awkward nearby fact. Wisdom is the two kept in conversation. | B:
MUNDANE: Learning, messaging, siblings and the neighbourhood at one pole; higher study, travel, publishing and belief at the other — how a person both gossips and preaches. | B:
ENERGY: A branching, distributing current: quick local signal at the 3rd, long trajectory at the 9th, refreshed by movement. | B:
MINIMAL: Near mind, far mind. The 3rd collects; the 9th means. Cadent — connects and passes on. | B:
MARKERS: near, far, cadent | ASK: Where has a cherished belief (9th) tripped over an inconvenient fact close to home (3rd)?

## axis-h4-h10 · house-axis
KERNEL: The 4th and 10th are the angular axis of roots and role — the vertical of the chart, the parental spine. The 4th house (personal, the foundation) is home, family, the inner base; the 10th (transpersonal, the summit) is career, reputation, what the world holds you to. Each completes the other: the 10th is built on what the 4th laid down, and the 4th is where the public self goes to be a private person. Angular — load-bearing and lifelong.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The mother-world of the 4th and the father-world of the 10th (whichever parent carried which) set the terms of belonging and demand; the task is a life where being held and being answerable no longer war. | B:
MUNDANE: Home, family and origins at one pole; career, public standing and authority at the other. Time and loyalty given to one are felt as a debt at the other. | B:
ENERGY: A vertical current from base to summit — deep, structural, slow; what is founded at the 4th sets how high the 10th can bear to rise. | B:
MINIMAL: Roots and role, the parental spine. The 4th holds; the 10th builds. Angular — lifelong. | B:
MARKERS: roots, role, angular | ASK: How does the home you came from still set the ceiling — or the terms — of your public ambition?

## axis-h5-h11 · house-axis
KERNEL: The 5th and 11th are the succedent axis of creation and belonging — what I make as myself, and what I join beyond myself. The 5th house (personal) is play, romance, children, the work signed in my own hand; the 11th (transpersonal) is friends, groups, causes, the future held in common. Each completes the other: the 11th keeps the 5th's self-expression from mere display, and the 5th keeps the 11th's ideals warm and particular. Succedent — it invests and sustains.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The heart's need to create as a singular self meets, in the 11th, the need to belong among equals; the performer and the citizen learn to share one life. | B:
MUNDANE: Romance, creativity, play and children at one pole; friendships, networks, groups and hopes at the other — what one makes alone, and what one joins. | B:
ENERGY: A radiant, sustaining current: personal fire at the 5th fed outward into the shared grid of the 11th. | B:
MINIMAL: Made by me, held in common. The 5th creates; the 11th belongs. Succedent — sustains. | B:
MARKERS: creation, belonging, succedent | ASK: Does what you make for yourself (5th) feed the groups you belong to (11th), or compete with them?

## axis-h6-h12 · house-axis
KERNEL: The 6th and 12th are the cadent axis of order and dissolution — the work of keeping a life, and the letting-go of it. The 6th house (personal) is routine, health, service, the day made workable; the 12th (transpersonal) is retreat, the unconscious, what is surrendered or dissolved into something larger. Each completes the other: the 12th is the rest and mystery the 6th's diligence needs, and the 6th is the form that keeps the 12th from formless drift. Cadent — it adapts, serves, and dissolves.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Discriminating daily work meets, in the 12th, the undertow that dissolves all distinctions — illness, retreat, the collective deep; competence learns it cannot do everything, and surrender learns it needs a form. | B:
MUNDANE: Routine, health, employment and service at one pole; retreat, hospitals, the hidden and the given-up at the other — the workable day and the hours outside every budget. | B:
ENERGY: A filtering current that finally opens to the sea: order refined at the 6th, boundaries dissolved at the 12th. | B:
MINIMAL: Order and ocean. The 6th tends; the 12th dissolves. Cadent — serves, then surrenders. | B:
MARKERS: order, dissolution, cadent | ASK: What routine (6th) keeps you afloat — and what happens when you cannot retreat (12th)?

## house-1 · house-inflection
KERNEL: The 1st house is the angular, personal pole of the 1st–7th axis: the body, the manner, how life is met at the door — the mask that is also the face. Its complement is the 7th, the other whose response completes the picture.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The 1st house is the emerging self, identity as first gesture; whatever it cannot include is handed to the 7th-house other until it is recognised as one's own. | B:
MUNDANE: Appearance, physical vitality and the first impression you make — the approach others meet before they meet you. | B:
ENERGY: An angular, self-starting current at the eastern horizon: whatever sits here colours everything downstream and is shown at once. | B:
MINIMAL: Body, manner, approach. Angular, personal. The 7th completes it. | B:
MARKERS: self, body, angular | ASK: What do people meet first in you — and is it the whole of you?

## house-2 · house-inflection
KERNEL: The 2nd house is the succedent, personal pole of the 2nd–8th axis: money, possessions, the body's needs, and the felt sense of one's own worth. Its complement is the 8th — what is shared, owed and transformed.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The 2nd house asks what is truly mine and what I am worth without anyone's leave; its shadow is worth confused with what is owned, which the 8th later strips back. | B:
MUNDANE: Earnings, belongings, resources and the security they buy — what you can hold in your own two hands. | B:
ENERGY: A succedent, accumulating current that steadies what the 1st house began: it banks, and resists being moved. | B:
MINIMAL: What's mine, and my worth. Succedent, personal. The 8th shares it. | B:
MARKERS: worth, possessions, succedent | ASK: Where does your sense of worth still rest on what you own rather than who you are?

## house-3 · house-inflection
KERNEL: The 3rd house is the cadent, personal pole of the 3rd–9th axis: speech, siblings, short journeys, the learning of the immediate world. Its complement is the 9th — the far mind that seeks the meaning behind the facts.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The 3rd house is the mind before it commits — curious, plural, gathering; its neglected pole is the 9th's single meaning, which returns as a hunger for something to believe. | B:
MUNDANE: Conversation, messages, siblings, neighbours, errands and the everyday commute — the near traffic of information. | B:
ENERGY: A cadent, branching current: many quick channels, low load on each, refreshed by exchange. | B:
MINIMAL: Speech, siblings, the near. Cadent, personal. The 9th means it. | B:
MARKERS: communication, learning, cadent | ASK: Which of your many interests would you least like to have to give up?

## house-4 · house-inflection
KERNEL: The 4th house is the angular, personal pole of the 4th–10th axis: home, family, ancestry, the inner foundation a life is built on. Its complement is the 10th — the public role that rests on this base.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The 4th house is the root and the mother-ground of the psyche, the felt past; its disowned pole is the 10th's exposure, and it returns as the pull to withdraw from the world's demand. | B:
MUNDANE: Home, property, family origins and the private life behind closed doors — where you come from and return to. | B:
ENERGY: An angular, foundational current at the base of the chart: deep and load-bearing, felt more than seen. | B:
MINIMAL: Home, family, foundations. Angular, personal. The 10th builds on it. | B:
MARKERS: home, roots, angular | ASK: What from your family foundation are you still building on — or still bracing against?

## house-5 · house-inflection
KERNEL: The 5th house is the succedent, personal pole of the 5th–11th axis: creativity, romance, play, children — whatever is made and signed in one's own hand. Its complement is the 11th — the wider group the personal gift is offered to.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The 5th house is the heart declaring itself, the self at play; its shadow is display for its own sake, which the 11th cools into something shared. | B:
MUNDANE: Romance, creative work, sport, gambling and children — the arenas where you express and enjoy yourself openly. | B:
ENERGY: A succedent, radiant current: personal warmth generated and held at a centre, broadcast outward. | B:
MINIMAL: Play, romance, creation. Succedent, personal. The 11th shares it. | B:
MARKERS: creativity, play, succedent | ASK: What do you create purely because it is yours — and who eventually gets to enjoy it?

## house-6 · house-inflection
KERNEL: The 6th house is the cadent, personal pole of the 6th–12th axis: work, routine, service and health — the daily craft of keeping a life in order. Its complement is the 12th — the retreat and dissolution that order alone cannot provide.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The 6th house is the self refined through useful work and self-correction; its exiled pole is the 12th's surrender, and overwork returns as the exhaustion that finally forces rest. | B:
MUNDANE: Employment, daily routine, diet, health and the small maintenance that keeps a life running. | B:
ENERGY: A cadent, filtering current: it sorts and improves the flow, precise and easily overloaded. | B:
MINIMAL: Work, routine, health. Cadent, personal. The 12th dissolves it. | B:
MARKERS: work, health, cadent | ASK: What are you still perfecting through effort that might actually need rest?

## house-7 · house-inflection
KERNEL: The 7th house is the angular, transpersonal pole of the 1st–7th axis: partnership, marriage, and the declared opponent — the other met across the horizon. Its complement is the 1st — the self one brings to every meeting.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The 7th house is the encountered other, and the screen for everything the 1st house won't own; the marriage or the enemy carries back the disowned self for collection. | B:
MUNDANE: Marriage, business partners, close allies and open enemies — the one-to-one relationships you enter by agreement. | B:
ENERGY: An angular, relational current at the western horizon: it activates on contact, charged by the presence of another. | B:
MINIMAL: Partner, opponent, mirror. Angular, transpersonal. The 1st brings the self. | B:
MARKERS: partnership, other, angular | ASK: What do your closest partnerships and your sharpest conflicts have in common?

## house-8 · house-inflection
KERNEL: The 8th house is the succedent, transpersonal pole of the 2nd–8th axis: shared resources, intimacy, death and transformation — what cannot be held alone. Its complement is the 2nd — the solid self that makes merging survivable.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The 8th house is where the ego meets what it cannot control — the other's power, desire, loss — and is remade; its shadow is control disguised as intimacy. | B:
MUNDANE: Joint finances, inheritance, debt, sexuality and crisis — the resources and bonds shared with another. | B:
ENERGY: A succedent, high-pressure current: it holds until it must discharge, transforming in depth rather than breadth. | B:
MINIMAL: Shared, intimate, transformed. Succedent, transpersonal. The 2nd grounds it. | B:
MARKERS: intimacy, transformation, succedent | ASK: Where has a loss or a merging of resources changed you more than any plan did?

## house-9 · house-inflection
KERNEL: The 9th house is the cadent, transpersonal pole of the 3rd–9th axis: travel, higher learning, belief and meaning — the far mind reaching past the immediate. Its complement is the 3rd — the near fact that keeps meaning honest.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The 9th house is the questing mind that wants life to signify; its neglected pole is the 3rd's patient particulars, which return as the fact the grand theory forgot. | B:
MUNDANE: Long journeys, foreign cultures, universities, law, publishing and religion — the wide horizons of experience. | B:
ENERGY: A cadent, long-arc current: launched at the horizon, impatient of detail, refreshed by distance. | B:
MINIMAL: Meaning, travel, belief. Cadent, transpersonal. The 3rd grounds it. | B:
MARKERS: belief, travel, cadent | ASK: What belief organises your life — and when did you last let a fact test it?

## house-10 · house-inflection
KERNEL: The 10th house is the angular, transpersonal pole of the 4th–10th axis: career, public standing, authority — the role the world holds you to. Its complement is the 4th — the private base the summit is built upon.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The 10th house is the self made public and accountable, the vocation and the parental expectation; its exiled pole is the 4th's need to be simply held, which surfaces at the top of the climb. | B:
MUNDANE: Career, reputation, public office and the authorities you answer to — how the world sees and ranks you. | B:
ENERGY: An angular, culminating current at the top of the chart: exposed, load-bearing, visible to all. | B:
MINIMAL: Career, standing, authority. Angular, transpersonal. The 4th is its base. | B:
MARKERS: career, authority, angular | ASK: Whose expectations are you meeting at the top — and were they ever yours?

## house-11 · house-inflection
KERNEL: The 11th house is the succedent, transpersonal pole of the 5th–11th axis: friends, groups, causes and the future held in common. Its complement is the 5th — the personal gift the group is offered.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The 11th house is belonging among equals and the ideals a self serves beyond itself; its shadow is the group used to avoid the exposure of the 5th's personal creation. | B:
MUNDANE: Friendships, networks, societies, campaigns and long-term hopes — the collective you throw your lot in with. | B:
ENERGY: A succedent, distributed current: stable across the whole network, cool at any single node. | B:
MINIMAL: Friends, groups, hopes. Succedent, transpersonal. The 5th makes it personal. | B:
MARKERS: friendship, groups, succedent | ASK: Which group carries your hopes — and does it leave room for you to be singular?

## house-12 · house-inflection
KERNEL: The 12th house is the cadent, transpersonal pole of the 6th–12th axis: retreat, the unconscious, and what is surrendered or dissolved into something larger. Its complement is the 6th — the daily form that keeps surrender from becoming drift.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The 12th house is the dissolving boundary — the collective deep, what is hidden or renounced; its missing pole is the 6th's discrimination, and unbounded surrender returns as the need for a form to hold it. | B:
MUNDANE: Retreat, hospitals, institutions, the hidden and the given-up — the hours and matters outside ordinary account. | B:
ENERGY: A cadent, oceanic current: no fixed banks, everything eventually connected, restorative and blurring. | B:
MINIMAL: Retreat, the hidden, surrender. Cadent, transpersonal. The 6th gives it form. | B:
MARKERS: retreat, surrender, cadent | ASK: Where do you go to dissolve — and what form keeps that from pulling you under?
