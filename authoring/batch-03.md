## aspect-sun-moon-neutral · aspect-pair
KERNEL: Sun–Moon: identity and instinct — what you are becoming and what you need to feel safe. In neutral (conjunction), the two functions fuse and act as one.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Will and need are fused at birth — a new-moon signature. Enormous self-coherence, but little inner counter-voice: other people must often supply the perspective the psyche does not generate internally. | B:
MUNDANE: Purpose and preference point the same way, giving strong momentum along one track. Second opinions are worth institutionalising, because the internal devil’s advocate is quiet. | B:
ENERGY: Both primary currents surge and ebb together, like a spring tide: high highs, low lows, and a fresh-start quality after each trough. The cycle rewards clean beginnings. | B:
MINIMAL: Wants and needs are the same thing. Strong momentum, weak internal cross-check. | B:
MARKERS: identity, need, neutral | ASK: When what you want and what you need disagree, which usually wins?
