# Authoring batch 04 — Wave 5 — synastry probe (dual contacts, read from both sides)

Marks: ✓ lands · ~ flat · ✗ wrong/preachy. React, don't analyse.

FORMAT PROBE: every reading below states BOTH sides of the contact.
If that shape works, the rest of the synastry wave is authored the same way.

## syn-sun-moon-neutral · synastry-pair
KERNEL: One person's Sun conjunct the other's Moon: purpose and instinct on the same degree. The Sun person supplies direction, the Moon person supplies climate — the contact is not symmetrical, so it is read from both sides.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The Sun person's purpose and the Moon person's instinct occupy the same ground — the oldest recognition there is, and it feels like coming home. For the Moon person the other becomes safety itself: easy to orbit, hard to see clearly. For the Sun person, being someone's weather is flattering until it turns into a duty never actually agreed to. | B:
MUNDANE: Domestic life assembles itself here without much discussion: the Moon person keeps the home that the Sun person's plans quietly assume. The drift to watch is the Moon person's needs going unspoken because the Sun person's direction has become the direction. | B:
ENERGY: Two currents in phase and pulling the same way — purpose and instinct firing as one, low friction and high draw. The risk is resonance without correction: nothing in the system is shaped to say stop. | B:
MINIMAL: Sun on Moon: instant belonging. The Moon person feels held; the Sun person sets the course. Easy — and easy to stop noticing. | B:
MARKERS: belonging, direction, neutral | ASK: Which of you sets the weather in that relationship, and which of you lives in it?

## syn-moon-pluto-neutral · synastry-pair
KERNEL: One person's Moon conjunct the other's Pluto: need fused with depth and power. The Moon person is the one exposed; the Pluto person is the one who sees. Read from both sides.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Need and depth fuse. The Pluto person reads the Moon person's hidden weather without being told, which arrives first as being finally known and later as being occupied. The Moon person opens what they cannot easily close again; the Pluto person, who intended no surveillance, finds they cannot un-know it. | B:
MUNDANE: Intense from the first hour and hard to leave — the bond that outlasts what ought to end it, for better and worse. The Moon person ends up more exposed than they planned; the Pluto person ends up knowing things they were never told. Privacy has to be negotiated out loud, because by default there will not be any. | B:
ENERGY: A tidal current drawn into a compressed one: enormous depth, no shallows, and regeneration through crisis rather than through rest. This pairing does not do casual. | B:
MINIMAL: Moon on Pluto: known to the bone. The Moon person opens; the Pluto person cannot un-see. Binding — needs deliberate boundaries. | B:
MARKERS: depth, exposure, neutral | ASK: Who in that pairing knows more than they were told — and how did it feel to be the other one?

## syn-venus-mars-flowing · synastry-pair
KERNEL: One person's Venus trine or sextile the other's Mars: value and desire cooperate. The Mars person moves, the Venus person welcomes — different experiences of the same ease.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: Desire and value run in one channel without shorting: the Mars person's wanting is received as charm rather than pressure, and the Venus person's warmth reads as invitation rather than obligation. Rare — and easy to under-rate precisely because nothing hurts. | B:
MUNDANE: Attraction that works in practice: courtship, taste and timing agree, the Mars person moves and the Venus person welcomes it, and neither has to explain themselves. Nothing here forces growth, so it can quietly idle. | B:
ENERGY: Active and receptive currents phase-matched — build and release cycling smoothly, sustainable heat rather than flare and ash. | B:
MINIMAL: Venus–Mars flowing: the Mars person moves, the Venus person welcomes, and it works. Warm, easy, uneventful — nothing forces either to change. | B:
MARKERS: attraction, ease, flowing | ASK: Was the ease of that attraction something you noticed at the time, or only afterwards?

## syn-sun-venus-flowing · synastry-pair
KERNEL: One person's Sun trine or sextile the other's Venus: identity meets liking. The Venus person appreciates; the Sun person is appreciated — a pleasant asymmetry worth naming.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The Venus person likes the Sun person as they actually are rather than as a project, and being liked for one's plain self is quietly disarming. For the Sun person it is easy to bask; for the Venus person, admiration can slide into arranging oneself around someone else's light. | B:
MUNDANE: Straightforwardly pleasant: shared taste, easy company, low maintenance. The Venus person makes the Sun person's world more agreeable, the Sun person gives the Venus person something to admire. Watch the slow drift over who is the occasion and who is the setting. | B:
ENERGY: A radiant current meeting a receptive one, well matched — warmth given, warmth received, very little lost in transmission. | B:
MINIMAL: Sun–Venus: easy liking. The Venus person appreciates; the Sun person shines. Pleasant — watch who becomes the audience. | B:
MARKERS: liking, ease, flowing | ASK: In that pairing, who was the occasion and who was the setting?

## syn-moon-saturn-challenge · synastry-pair
KERNEL: One person's Moon square or opposite the other's Saturn: need meets limit. The Moon person carries the feeling, the Saturn person the structure — and the two live it very differently.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The Moon person meets, in the Saturn person, the old measuring eye — the one that assessed before it comforted. The Saturn person meanwhile carries an unasked-for responsibility for someone else's weather and answers it with structure, because tenderness was never modelled for them either. Neither is doing it on purpose. | B:
MUNDANE: The Moon person asks for reassurance and hears a correction; the Saturn person offers reliability and is told it is cold. Both are giving what they have. It improves markedly once the Saturn person says the warm thing out loud and the Moon person names the need instead of testing for it. | B:
ENERGY: A containing current laid across a tidal one: feeling rises, meets a wall that does not move, builds pressure and discharges late. Real endurance lives here — once the dam is rebuilt as a bank rather than a barrier. | B:
MINIMAL: Moon–Saturn: the Moon person feels judged; the Saturn person feels burdened. Not malice — different equipment. Ages well if named. | B:
MARKERS: need, limit, challenge | ASK: When you asked that person for reassurance, what did you actually get — and what did you think it meant?

## syn-venus-saturn-challenge · synastry-pair
KERNEL: One person's Venus square or opposite the other's Saturn: affection meets caution and duty. The Venus person feels rationed; the Saturn person believes they are being serious. Read from both sides.
PSYCHOLOGICAL: The Venus person offers affection and finds it weighed. The Saturn person, who trusts only what endures, cannot say yes lightly and therefore seems to be saying no. Love here is asked to prove it will last before it is permitted to be enjoyed. | B:
MUNDANE: The Venus person feels rationed; the Saturn person feels the relationship is finally being taken seriously. Commitment is genuinely on offer — it simply arrives dressed as reluctance. Explicit terms help far more than reassurance does. | B:
ENERGY: A warm current meeting a resistor: flow is throttled and what passes through is concentrated. Slow to charge, durable once charged. | B:
MINIMAL: Venus–Saturn: the Venus person feels rationed; the Saturn person is being careful, not cold. Slow, durable, needs saying aloud. | B:
MARKERS: affection, caution, challenge | ASK: Did that carefulness read to you as commitment or as coldness — and did you ever ask which it was?
